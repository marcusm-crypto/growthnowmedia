## The Challenge

Argyle Cryo needed booked appointments, not more leads to chase. Lead volume looks good on a report, but it does not fill a calendar. The job was to turn ad spend into confirmed, deposit backed appointments that actually showed up.

## What We Did

We ran the Growth Now Method: one offer built to convert (a CryoSlimming offer), direct response ads to put it in front of the right people, and our team following up on every lead for a full seven days. Every appointment was booked with a deposit at the time of booking, so the calendar filled with people who had committed. Argyle Cryo's only job was to close.

## The Results

$3,659 in ad spend produced 51 bookings in 30 days, every one secured with a deposit, at a $38 average cost per booking, collecting $27,000 in revenue.

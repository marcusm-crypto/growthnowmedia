## The Challenge

Midwest Wellness Lounge needed booked appointments, not more leads to chase. Lead volume looks good on a report, but it does not fill a calendar. The job was to turn ad spend into confirmed, deposit backed appointments that actually showed up, and then keep it running month after month.

## What We Did

We ran the Growth Now Method: one offer built to convert (a CryoSlimming offer), direct response ads to put it in front of the right people, and our team following up on every lead for a full seven days. Every appointment was booked with a deposit at the time of booking, so the calendar filled with people who had committed. Midwest Wellness Lounge's only job was to close.

## The Results

The playbook below is the first 30 days: $3,268 in ad spend produced 56 deposit backed bookings and $21,000 in collected revenue. We kept the system running, and over five months it produced 236 bookings and $75,125 in revenue on $15,543 of ad spend.

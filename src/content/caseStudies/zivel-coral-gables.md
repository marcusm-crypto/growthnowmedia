## The Challenge

Zivel Coral Gables runs CryoSlimming in a market crowded with contouring options. The job was not lead volume. It was booked consults that showed up and bought, without discounting the brand into the ground.

## What We Did

We built the campaign around a $199 CryoSlim special and a $99 CryoFacial offer, positioned against the gym and surgery alternatives their clients were already weighing. Direct response ads drove to an optimized landing page built to turn clicks into calls and calls into confirmed appointments. Our team followed up on every lead and booked them with a deposit, so each of the 11 bookings represented someone who had already committed money, not a maybe.

## The Results

$640 in ad spend produced 11 deposited bookings in 13 days at a $16.98 cost per lead. The clinic collected over $13,000 in that window from the two offers. Fewer, heavier bookings that showed and bought beat a pile of leads that never would have.

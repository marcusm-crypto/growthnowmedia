## The Challenge

Ageless Aesthetics needed booked appointments, not more leads to chase. Lead volume looks good on a report, but it does not fill a calendar. The job was to turn ad spend into confirmed, deposit backed appointments that actually showed up.

## What We Did

We ran the Growth Now Method: one offer built to convert (a T-Shape special), direct response ads to put it in front of the right people, and our team following up on every lead for a full seven days. Every appointment was booked with a deposit at the time of booking, so the calendar filled with people who had committed. Ageless Aesthetics's only job was to close.

## The Results

$396 in ad spend produced 16 bookings in 7 days, every one secured with a $29 deposit, at a $24.75 average cost per booking, positioned to generate $6,000 or more in revenue.

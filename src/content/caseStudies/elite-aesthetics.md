## The Challenge

Elite Aesthetics had T-Shape capacity sitting open. Like most contouring clinics, the gap was not demand for the treatment. It was consistent consult flow, and making sure the people who booked actually walked in.

## What We Did

We built the campaign around a $159 T-Shape special and ran direct response ads against it. Every lead form asked a qualifying question, because lead volume that never books is a vanity number. Our team followed up on every lead, booked them straight into the clinic's calendar, and collected a deposit at booking. The clinic's only job was to run the consults.

## The Results

$682 in ad spend produced 56 deposited bookings in 14 days at a $5.96 cost per lead. The clinic collected $26,450 in that window. Every booking came in with a deposit attached, which is why the calendar held instead of leaking.

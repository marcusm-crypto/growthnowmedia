## The Challenge

A brand new location has no client base, no reviews, and one shot at opening weekend. The Covery Lafayette needed a packed grand opening, not a soft one, and they needed the weekend to produce members and package buyers, not just foot traffic.

## What We Did

We ran four campaigns at once, each with a different job. Low barrier offers packed the facility across every service line. Offers were built around the clients the location actually wanted to keep, not just bodies through the door. Package and membership incentives were structured into the weekend from day one, because openings live or die on what people commit to before they leave. Our team followed up on every lead and booked them into the calendar before the doors opened.

## The Results

$1,218 in ad spend produced 116 bookings at an $11.39 average cost per lead. The location collected $43,000 during the 4 day Grand Opening weekend. They opened with a full calendar and a member base instead of an empty lobby.

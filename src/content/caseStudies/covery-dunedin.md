## The Challenge

An established location does not get to coast on a special event. The Covery Dunedin's 2 year anniversary was a window to reactivate past clients, pull in new ones, and sell packages, and windows like that close fast. The event needed offers engineered for revenue, not a party that felt busy and produced nothing.

## What We Did

We ran four anniversary campaigns simultaneously across their service lines. Each offer was built around the clients the location wanted to sell, with low barrier entry points to pack the facility and package and membership incentives layered on top. Our team handled the lead follow-up and booked every appointment into the calendar ahead of the weekend, so the event opened with a full schedule instead of hoping for walk-ins.

## The Results

$1,955 in ad spend produced 86 bookings at a $16.16 average cost per lead. The location collected $41,000 across the anniversary weekend. One event window, planned right, outproduced a normal month.
